# **App Name**: ZAY Atelier

## Core Features:

- Premium Product Display: Browse a curated catalog of high-end apparel with elegant product cards and intuitive filtering for a sophisticated shopping experience.
- Immersive Product Details: Explore products with high-resolution imagery, detailed specifications, zoom functionality, and precise variation selection (size, color) before adding to cart.
- Intuitive Shopping Cart: Manage selections with a dynamic shopping cart experience allowing easy addition, review, quantity adjustments, and removal of items.
- Seamless User Authentication: Securely register and log in to manage personal details, view order history, and maintain a wishlist for a personalized customer space.
- Streamlined Checkout Process: Complete purchases efficiently and securely through an elegant, multi-step checkout optimized for conversion and mobile UX.
- AI Style Guide Tool: Receive personalized product recommendations and complementary outfit ideas using an intelligent AI tool, enhancing the luxury shopping journey and discovery.
- Essential Product Management: An administrative interface for ZAY staff to easily add, edit, or remove product listings with their full details, enabling dynamic catalog management.

## Style Guidelines:

- Main background: A soft, warm off-white (#FAF7F4) chosen to convey luxury, cleanliness, and serve as an elegant canvas for premium content.
- Primary action and accent: A sophisticated, vibrant rose (#D4537E, designated 'Primary Rose') used sparingly for compelling call-to-actions and key interactive elements.
- Secondary accent: A soft nude tone (#C4927A, designated 'Nude Secondary') providing elegant contrast and warmth for complementary elements and secondary highlights.
- Subtle UI background tones: Lighter rose (#F4C0D1, designated 'Rose Light') and pale rose (#FBEAF0, designated 'Rose Pale Background') are employed for delicate backgrounds, active states, and soft UI components.
- Main text: A rich, deep dark brown (#2C1A14, designated 'Main Text') chosen for superior readability and a distinguished, premium textual presence.
- Muted text and secondary elements: A warm, medium brown (#8A7068, designated 'Muted Text') used for subordinate text, descriptive details, and to maintain an overall elegant textual hierarchy.
- Borders and dividers: A light, understated beige (#E8DDD6, designated 'Border') for clean visual separation and refined element distinction without harshness.
- Headline font: 'Cormorant Garamond' (serif) for an editorial, luxurious, and highly elegant title display, commanding immediate attention. Body and UI text font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for modern clarity, optimal readability, and a clean interface across all devices, ensuring accessibility. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Utilize minimal, elegant, and modern line icons from Lucide React, thoughtfully integrated to maintain a clean, sophisticated, and intuitive visual language across the platform.
- Absolute mobile-first responsive design, emphasizing fluid transitions, a highly premium sticky header with thoughtful micro-interactions, and a beautiful bottom navigation on mobile for an uncompromised, intuitive UX.
- An exceptional homepage with an editorial, high-fashion aesthetic featuring a visually compelling hero section, curated product displays, and engaging visual storytelling through lookbooks. Product grids are clean, allowing each item to stand out.
- Sophisticated, subtle animations using Framer Motion including smooth page transitions, elegant fade-ins for sections, gentle reveal-on-scroll effects, and refined hover interactions (e.g., luxury product card hover, image hover zoom) for a premium and 'wow' experience.
- Interactive elements feature understated floating effects, animated feedback for wishlist hearts and cart badges, and intelligent elevation for CTA hovers to enhance micro-interactions and fluidity without over-animation.
- Optimized loading states with refined skeleton loaders and seamlessly animated modal and drawer transitions to ensure a consistently fluid, professional, and rapid user experience.
